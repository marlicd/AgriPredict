1. What is the problem you are solving?

>Farmers and agricultural planners often make decisions without knowing how environmental conditions will affect crop yield. My project uses historical agricultural and weather data to predict crop yield and help people make better farming decisions.

2. What tools did you use?

>I used Python for the project. I used Pandas and NumPy for data cleaning, Matplotlib and Seaborn for visualizations, and Scikit-learn for machine learning. I used Linear Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost to predict and compare crop yields. I also plan to use Streamlit to create a simple dashboard for making predictions.

3. What insights do you want to discover / What solutions do you want to offer?

>I want to find out which factors, such as rainfall, temperature, fertilizer, crop type, and region, have the biggest effect on crop yield. The goal is to build a system that can predict expected crop yield and help farmers and planners make better decisions about farming.

4. How would a business or community benefit from your work?

>Farmers could use the predictions to make better decisions about what and when to plant and how to use resources such as fertilizer and irrigation. Agricultural planners could also use the information to prepare for periods of low crop production and improve food-security planning.
